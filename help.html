Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fund my wallet? +
You can transfer directly to your dedicated account number from any banking application or USSD service. Funds reflect instantly.
What are the transaction limits? +
Tier 3 verified users can transfer up to ₦5,000,000 daily. You can upgrade your tier in the Profile settings by completing BVN/NIN verification.
How do virtual cards work? +
Virtual USD cards can be created and funded directly from your main wallet balance. Use them for international shopping, subscriptions, and online services.
What if a transaction fails? +
Failed transactions are automatically reversed within 24 hours. If your balance is not restored after 24 hours, contact us via Live Chat or Email with your transaction ID.
